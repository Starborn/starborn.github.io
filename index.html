About

I am an independent researcher and consultant, working on contract as analyst, subject matter expert, faculty and technical author. My research covers complexity management and sociotechnical systems. Current work focuses on AI knowledge representation, neurosymbolic AI and agent interoperability standards, backed by peer-reviewed publications and standards contributions.

Organisations hire me for systems analysis and specification, algorithmic audits, technical curricula and business intelligence advisories. I chair the W3C AI Knowledge Representation Community Group and lead research at the Epistemic Systems Lab of the Ronin Institute.
