Methods

Paola Di Maio, PhD · systems analysis and design, knowledge modelling, agent specification

I analyse and specify systems with established engineering methods, and adapt them to current problems, including the specification of AI agents. This page lists the methods I practise and teach, and the frameworks I have authored. Full method guides and templates are available to clients under contract.

Structured methods

Structured analysis and design

  • Structured analysis and structured design (SASD) in the DeMarco and Yourdon tradition: data flow diagrams, levelled specifications, coupling and cohesion.
  • Real-time extensions and their object-oriented successors in the Ward–Mellor and Shlaer–Mellor line, through to Executable UML.
  • Use case notation, object modelling and model-driven engineering, taught at graduate and postgraduate level.
Knowledge engineering

CommonKADS

  • Knowledge engineering and management with CommonKADS: organisation, task, agent, knowledge, communication and design models.
  • MAS-CommonKADS for multi-agent systems, and the communication model for interaction modelling between people and agents.
  • Ontology development and knowledge representation, from lightweight vocabularies to formal ontologies.
In development

Structured Agent Specification Method (SASM)

SASM adapts structured analysis artefacts to the design of AI agent systems, so that an agent's tasks, roles and hand-offs are specified and checked before the system is built. The method guide is in draft and is not published.

Frameworks

Authored frameworks

  • Knowledge Audit Framework (KAF), for auditing knowledge assets in engineering projects.
